Risk rules evaluated on end-of-day data in a fixed, documented order

How these rules are evaluated (EOD)
Each day: (1) legs entered before today are checked against today's high/low, SL before target, both inside the range = SL and the trade is flagged AMBIGUOUS; (2) complete square-off closes the other legs at today's close; (3) trailing SL moves from today's close; (4) SL-to-cost applies from the NEXT day; (5) a leg with re-entry left re-enters at today's close with its strike rule re-applied to today's data, and is checked from the next day (never on the exit day); (6) overall SL/target and then lock-profit use today's closing MTM; lock-profit arms on one close and can exit from the next close. Intraday order of events is unknown in EOD data, so none of these claim intraday accuracy.
